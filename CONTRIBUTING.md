# Contributing

Thanks for your interest! Contributions are welcome.

- Contributions are accepted under the project's [MIT license](LICENSE).
- Run `pre-commit install` before your first commit; hooks check formatting and scan for secrets.
- Never include real project names, email addresses, credentials or other personal data
  in code, tests, fixtures, screenshots or issues. Use `example.com` and fake names.
- Keep pull requests focused and describe what they change and why.
